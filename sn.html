Lingo Captions: mutemo wekuvanzika

Yakagadziridzwa kekupedzisira: 20 Mbudzi 2026

Lingo Captions inoshandura kutaura kuita zvinyorwa zvepasi, zvinyorwa zvakanyorwa nezvinoshandurwa zvakananga pachishandiso chako. Hapana account uye hapana kushambadzira, uye app haiteedzere kuti unoishandisa sei.

Lingo Captions haiunganidzi data yako

  • Muvandi haatambiri ruzha rwako, zvinyorwa zvako kana mifananidzo yako zvachose. Zvese zvaunoita muapp (kurekodha, zvinyorwa zvepasi, zvinyorwa zvakanyorwa, zvinoshandurwa, mashoko akachengetwa, madikishonari nezvigadziriso) zvinochengetwa muchengetedzo yeapp pachayo pachishandiso chako.
  • Makopi anoenda kunzvimbo dzako chete: faira yaunobudisa, kana backup yako yeiCloud kana Google. Mashoko ezvishandiso zvako ari pazasi.
  • Unogona kudzima hurukuro ipi neipi, pamwe nezvinyorwa zvayo zvakanyorwa nekurekodha kwayo, muHistory chero nguva. Kana ukabvisa app, zvose zvayakachengeta zvinoenda nayo.
  • App inoteerera chete kana uchishandisa zvinyorwa zvepasi, dictation kana kurekodha, uye inogara ichikuratidza kuti inoteerera rinhi.
  • Kurekodha kwako kwemaikorofoni pachako kunochengetwa kuitira kuti ukwanise kuvatamba zvekare. Kana usingadi kuvachengeta, dzima Chengeta kurekodha muSettings.
  • Kana app ichiisa zvinyorwa zvepasi pazvinonzwika zvemamwe maapps, ruzha irwo hurusingatambwi zvekare zvachose. Muguvaro reApp Store, Google Play neMicrosoft Store runochengetwa kwenguva pfupi chete, kuti zvinyorwa zvakanyorwa zvivandudzwe. Mashoko ezvishandiso zvako ari pazasi.
  • Budisa zvose (muHistory) inoisa zvinyorwa zvehurukuro dzako nemashoko akachengetwa mufaira rimwe, rinochengetwa kwaunosarudza. Mbozha inorivaraidza. Faira haina kurekodha, uye rinosiya chishandiso chako chete kana ukarichengeta kana kurinyorera kumwe.

Kana app ichishandisa internet

  • Kudhawunirodha: mamodheru emutauro, mazwi, madikishonari nemamodheru anoverenga zvinyorwa mumifananidzo anobva kumasaiti ruzhinji (Hugging Face, GitHub nesaiti yedikishonari yega yega), chete kana wavamba kana kubvuma kudhawunirodha. Sekudhawunirodha kupi nokupi, masaiti iwayo anoona kero yeinternet yechishandiso chako. App haitumire chinhu pamusoro pako kana zvawakagadzira.
  • Kuvandudzwa kwemamodheru: kamwe pazuva app inobvunza Hugging Face kana mamodheru emutauro awakadhawunirodha ane vhezheni itsva. Hugging Face inoona kero yako yeinternet nemafaira emamodheru akaongororwa. Unogona kuidzima muSettings.
  • Malinks aunonamira: app inodhawunirodha mhando dzenhau kubva kusaiti iyoyo, sezvaizoita browser.
  • Zvimiro zvechishandiso chako pachacho: kwazvinowanikwa pachishandiso chako, app inoshandisa kuziva kutaura, kushandura nezvimiro zvezvinyorwa zveApple kana Google pachavo, muchimiro chazvo chepachishandiso. Izvi ndchikamu chesystem yako uye zvinotevera mutemo wekuvanzika weApple kana Google.
  • Tuma Log (Ruzivisa dambudziko), muapps eWindows, Mac, iPhone neiPad: hapana chinotumwa kunze kwekunge wasarudza. Inovhura app yako yeemail iine malogs achangoburwa akabatanidzwa, uye iwe unotonga kana uchizvitumira. Malogs anoratidza vhezheni yeapp, chishandiso, zvakaitwa neapp nemakanganiso upi neupi. Haana kurekodha kana zvinyorwa zvakanyorwa, asi anogona kuve nemazita emafaira awakavhura.
  • Kutenga: kuedza kwemahara nekutenga zvinopfuura neApp Store, Google Play kana Microsoft Store. App inoziva chete kana wakatenga vhezheni yakazara; ruzivo rwako rwekubhadhara runogara nesitoro.

iPhone, iPad neMac

  • Ruzha rwunobva kumamwe maapps (Zvinyorwa zvepasi zve-system; paMac zvakare Zvinyorwa zvepasi zveboka muMafoni): paiPhone neiPad runodzimwa kana Vandudza yaishandisa, uye kwete gare gare pamaawa 24 kurekodha kwapera (kana kekutevera kwauchavhura app mushure meizvozvo). Vhezheni yeMac App Store inovandudza zvinyorwa zvakanyorwa pachayo kana zvinyorwa zvepasi zvamira, ipapo inodzima ruzha.
  • Backup yeHistory yeiCloud, chete kana wairovera (iPhone, iPad nevhezheni yeMac App Store): zvinyorwa zvehurukuro dzako nemashoko akachengetwa zvinokopwa kufolda yeapp muiCloud Drive yako pachako, kuitira kuti iPhone, iPad kana Mac yako imwe ine Apple ID imwechete izvitambire zvakare. Kurekodha, mamodheru nezvigadziriso hazvikopwi zvachose. Apple inochengeta mafaira aya maererano nemutemo wayo wekuvanzika, uye muvandi haazvitambiri zvachose. Kana ukadzima backup muSettings, kukopa kunomira; mafaira aripo kare muiCloud Drive anogara ipapo kusvikira wadzimisa.
  • Backup yeiPhone kana iPad yako: backup yako pachako yeiCloud kana kombiyuta inosanganisira data yeapp, sedzemamwe maapps, kunze kwekurekodha kwako nemamodheru emutauro. Unogona kudhawunirodha mamodheru zvekare mushure mekudzoreredza.

Android

  • Ruzha rwunobva kumamwe maapps (Zvinyorwa zvepasi ze-system): runodzimwa kana Vandudza yaishandisa, uye kwete gare gare pamaawa 24 kurekodha kwapera (kana kekutevera kwauchavhura app mushure meizvozvo).
  • Kushandura nekuverenga zvinyorwa mumifananidzo nemuPDF zvinoshandisa ML Kit yeGoogle, inoshanda pachishandiso chako. Ruzha rwako, zvinyorwa zvako nemifananidzo yako zvinogara pachishandiso chako. Apo zvimiro izvi zvichishanda, ML Kit inotumira kuGoogle ruzivo rwehunyanzvi: nhamba dzekushanda, makodhi emakanganiso, chishandiso chako nevhezheni yeapp, nemitauro yaunoshandura pakati payo, nenhamba yekuzivikanwa yekopi iyi yeapp. Inotumirwa yakavharirwa, uye Google inoti haiipi vamwe. Mamodheru ekushandura eML Kit anodhawunirodhwa kubva kuGoogle. Google inozvitsanangura pano: Kuburitswa kwedata yeML Kit.
  • Kuchinjira kufoni itsva: kana ukachinjira nekutamisa kubva kufoni kuenda kufoni kwauri iwe waunotanga, uye chishandiso chekutamisa chefoni chichichitsigira, zvose zvinochengetwa neapp zvinouya newe, kunze kwemamodheru emutauro nemamodheru anoverenga zvinyorwa mumifananidzo, aunogona kudhawunirodha zvekare.
  • Backup kuaccount yako yeGoogle: History yako nemashoko akachengetwa zvinosanganisirwa chete kana backup iyoyo yakavharirwa kubva kumucheto kusvika kumucheto, izvo zvinoda kukiya sikirini pafoni yako. Kurekodha, mamodheru, madikishonari nezvigadziriso hazvisanganisirwi zvachose. Google inochengeta backup maererano nemutemo wayo wekuvanzika, uye muvandi haatambiri zvachose. Unogona kudzima backup yeGoogle muzvigadziriso zveAndroid.
  • Hapana Tuma Log paAndroid. Panzvimbo payo, kana ukagovana data yekushandisa nekuongorora neGoogle, Google Play inoratidza muvandi mishumo yekuparara: vhezheni yeapp, chishandiso nenzvimbo mune app payakaparara.

Windows

  • Ruzha rwunobva kumamwe maapps (Zvinyorwa zvepasi ze-system, uye Zvinyorwa zvepasi zveboka muMafoni, zvinoruvhenganisa nemaikorofoni yako), muvhezheni yeMicrosoft Store: runochengetwa kuti zvinyorwa zvakanyorwa zvivandudzwe nekusiyanisa vanotaura (Ziva Vanotaura). Runodzimwa kana ose maviri aruishandisa, uye kwete gare gare pamaawa 24 kurekodha kwapera (kana kekutevera kwauchavhura app mushure meizvozvo).

Vana

  • App haiunganidzi ruzivo rwemunhu kubva kunaani zvake, vana vachisanganisirwa.

Kurekodha vamwe vanhu

  • Ndapota ziva vanhu usati warekodha kana kuisa zvinyorwa zvepasi pahurukuro newo. Mitemo yakasiyana kubva kunyika kuenda kunyika.

Shanduko

  • Kana mutemo uyu ukachinja, muvandi achaparadzira vhezheni itsva papeji iyi, nezuva rayo.
