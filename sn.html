Lingo Captions: mutemo wekuvanzika

Yakagadziridzwa kekupedzisira: 13 Mbudzi 2026

Lingo Captions inoshandura kutaura kuita zvinyorwa zviri pasi pavhidhiyo, zvakanyorwa netudziro pachishandiso chako pachako. App haina account, haina zvirevo zvekushambadza uye haina kuongorora kwayo pachayo, uye mugadziri haawani chero chinhu chemazwi, zvinyorwa kana mifananidzo yako.

Zvinoramba zviri pachishandiso chako

  • Marekodhi ako, zvinyorwa zviri pasi pavhidhiyo, zvakanyorwa, dudziro, mazwi akachengetwa, madhikishinari uye zvirongwa zvako zvinochengetwa mundima yekuchengeta yeapp pachayo pachishandiso chako.
  • Unogona kudzima nhaurirano, pamwe nezvakanyorwa zvayo nerekodhi rayo, pachishandiso chako muNhoroondo chero nguva. Kubvisa app kunobvisa zvese zvainge yachengeta pachishandiso chako.
  • Maikorofoni inoshandiswa chete panguva yaunoshandisa zvinyorwa zviri pasi pavhidhiyo, dictation kana rekodhi, uye app inoratidza painenge ichiteerera.
  • Marekodhi ako emaikorofoni anochengetwa kuti ugone kuaridza zvakare; Chengeta marekodhi muZvirongwa inodzima izvi.
  • Muushanduro dzeApp Store, Google Play neMicrosoft Store, ruzha rwunobatwa kubva kune dzimwe app (Zvinyorwa zvesystem, uye zvinyorwa zvePoka kana zveMafoni, zvinosanganisa ruzha nemaikorofoni) runochengetwa chete kuitira kuti app igone kunatsa zvakanyorwa zvayo uye, paWindows, kuziva vanotaura vayo, uye runoridzwa zvakare hapana. PaiPhone, iPad neAndroid runodzimwa pakarepo kana Natsa yaishandisa, uye paWindows kana Natsa neZiva Vanotaura zvese zvaishandisa; kana zvisiri izvo maawa makumi maviri nemana mushure mekunge rekodhi yapera, kana kekutanga app painoshanda mushure meizvozvo. Ushanduro yeMac App Store inonatsa zvakanyorwa pachayo kana zvinyorwa zviri pasi pavhidhiyo zvamira, ozodzima ruzha.
  • Android 12 nedzakanyanya kutsva chete: paunoenda kufoni itsva uchishandisa kuendesa kubva kuchishandiso kuenda kuchimwe chishandiso kwaunotanga, uye chishandiso chefoni chekuendesa chichikutsigira, zvese zvinochengetwa neapp zvinoenda newe; mamodhel ekutaura nemamodhel anoverenga zvinyorwa zviri mumifananidzo chete ndiwo anosara, uye anogona kudhawunirodhwa zvakare. paAndroid 10 ne11 data yeapp haiendeswi. Data yeapp haimbosanganisirwi mu-cloud backup yeAndroid.
  • PaiPhone neiPad, zvese zvinochengetwa neapp zvinosanganisirwa mubackup yako pachako yeiCloud kana yekombiyuta, sedata yemamwe maapp; marekodhi ako nemamodhel ekutaura chete ndizvo zvisingasanganisirwi, uye mamodhel anogona kudhawunirodhwa zvakare mushure mekudzorera.

Zvinobuda pachishandiso chako, uye painobuda

  • Madhawunirodhi aunotanga kana aunobvumira: mamodhel ekutaura, mazwi, madhikishinari nemamodhel anoverenga zvinyorwa zviri mumifananidzo zvinouya kumaseva ruzhinji (Hugging Face, GitHub neWebsite yedhikishinari yega yega pachayo). Sezvazvakaita nedhawunirodhi ipi neipi, maseva iwayo anoona adhiresi yeinternet yechishandiso chako. App haitumi chinhu chipi nechipi pamusoro pako kana pamusoro pezvauinazvo.
  • Kamwe pazuva app inobvunza Hugging Face kuti mamodhel ekutaura aunodhawunirodha asanduka here, kuti ikwanise kupa zvigadziriso. Sezvazvakaita nedhawunirodhi ipi neipi, maseva anoona adhiresi yeinternet yechishandiso chako uye mafaira emamodhel anotariswa. Unogona kudzima ongororo iyi muzvirongwa zveapp.
  • Android chete: dudziro nekuverenga zvinyorwa zviri mumifananidzo nemuPDF zvinoshandisa ML Kit yeGoogle, inoshanda pachishandiso chako. Mazwi ako, zvinyorwa nemifananidzo zvinoramba zviri pachishandiso, asi panguva yezvinhu izvi zvichishanda, ML Kit inotumira Google ruzivo rwekuongorora, senhamba dzekushanda, makodhi emhosva, chishandiso neushanduro yeapp, mitauro yakasarudzwa yedudziro, uye chitupa chekuzivikanwa chekuiswa kweapp iyi. Inotumirwa yakavharirwa, uye Google inoti haipfuuri kune vamwe. Mamodhel edudziro eML Kit anodownlodhwa kubva kuGoogle. Google inotsanangura izvi pano: Kuburitswa kwedata yeML Kit.
  • Kana chishandiso chako chinazvo, app inoshandisa kuziva kutaura, dudziro nezvinhu zvezvinyorwa zveApple kana zveGoogle pachazvo munzira dzazvo dzepachishandiso. Izvi ndizvo zvikamu zvesystem uye zvinotevera mitemo yekuvanzika yeApple neGoogle.
  • Zvinongedzo zvaunonamira: app inodownlodha media kubva kusite yawataura, inoona chikumbiro sezvainge ichiona kubva kubrowser.
  • Tumira Log (Ripoti dambudziko), muapp dzeWindows, Mac, iPhone neiPad: chete kana wasarudza. Inovhura app yako pachako yeimeri ine ma log ekushanda achangoburwa akabatanidzwa, uye iwe unosarudza kuti uatumire here. Ma log anoti ushanduro ipi nechishandiso chipi zvakashanda uye zvakaitwa neapp, nemhosva dzipi zvadzo; haana marekodhi kana zvakanyorwa, asi anogona kutaura mazita emafaira awakavhura. App yeAndroid haina Tumira Log: Google Play inoratidza mugadziri mishumo yekuparara (ushanduro yeapp, chishandiso, uye panoparara muapp) kubva kuzvishandiso zvevaridzi vanogovana data yekushandisa nekuongorora neGoogle.
  • Kutenga: kuyedza kwahwu kwemahara nekutenga zvinodarika muGoogle Play, Microsoft Store kana App Store. App inongoziva kuti ushanduro yakazara yatengwa here; zvakadzama zvekubhadhara zvako zvinoramba zviri kuchitoro.

Vana

  • App haivhunganidzi ruzivo rwemunhu pachake kubva kuchero munhu, vana vachisanganisirwa.

Kurekodha vamwe vanhu

  • Zivisa vanhu usati warekodha kana kunyora zvinyorwa zviri pasi pavhidhiyo zvenhaurirano navo. Mitemo inosiyana nenyika.

Shanduko
